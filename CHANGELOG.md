# Changelog

## icq-nostalgia 0.1.0 (2026-11-14)

- Mint Messenger theme for the desktop app, light and dark
- Glossy title-bar backdrop with original drifting blossoms
- `Name (hh:mm):` headers: blue for you, orange for Claude
- Status flower above the prompt: Online, Occupied · typing…, Uh-oh! Claude needs you
- Original chime on permission prompts, questions, idle reminders and finished replies; bring your own `.wav` with `soundFile` or `sounds/custom.wav`
- Sound on macOS (afplay), Windows (PowerShell SoundPlayer) and Linux (paplay/aplay)
- `/icq test | mute | unmute | status`
- Workspace: `new-mod`, `check`, `dev-link` and `make-chime` scripts, starter template, CI on three OSes
