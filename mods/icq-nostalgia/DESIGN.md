# Mint Messenger: a design system from ICQ 6 (2007)

Derived from six ICQ 6 screenshots (conversation window, tabbed chat, options,
contact list, login) by sampling pixels, then tuned so text passes contrast in
the Claude desktop app. Tokens live in `hooks/design.ts`; the app palette is
built from them in `hooks/look.ts`.

## Principles

1. **Window, not page.** ICQ 6 was a glossy window: a green gradient title bar,
   a mint body, white message wells with 1 px aqua borders. The backdrop scene
   reproduces the bar and the mint; the app's panels become the wells.
2. **Names carry color, text does not.** Message text was plain black. Only the
   sender line was colored: blue for you, orange for the other person.
3. **Status is a flower.** Every contact had a small flower whose color was the
   status. We use an original six-petal `✿` (no eight-petal logo, no red petal).
4. **Soft, rounded, friendly.** Pill tabs (8 px), 3 px buttons, 6 px window
   corners, Tahoma 11 px.

| Online | `#5fb32a` | Claude is idle |
| Occupied · typing… | `#d9363e` | a turn is running |
| Uh-oh! Claude needs you | `#f07800` | permission prompt, question, idle reminder |

### Night mode ("ICQ at 2 a.m.")

Not an inversion: pine instead of mint. `surface-0..3` = `#0f1c16 #15261d
#1b2f24 #20352a`, text `#e6f7ec / #bcd9c7 / #97b8a5`. The scene's title bar
dims to a deep leaf gradient and the blossoms glow faintly.

| Button radius | 3 px |
| Border | 1 px |
| Title bar | 32 px |
| Tab height | 22 px |
| Spacing scale | 2 4 6 8 12 16 24 |
| Font | Tahoma, Verdana, Segoe UI; 11 px |

Mods can paint colors and a backdrop but cannot change the app's font or
radii, so shape and type tokens are documented here for anything drawn in a
scene or ported to CSS.

## Sound

`sounds/message.wav`: an original two-note chime (A5 then E6, soft bell
partials, 0.55 s, 16-bit mono 22 kHz) generated by `scripts/make-chime.mjs`.
It plays for permission prompts, Claude's multiple-choice questions, idle and
elicitation notifications, and finished replies longer than `minTurnSeconds`,
at most once per 2.5 s. A `.wav` you own can replace it via `soundFile` or
`sounds/custom.wav`.

## Trademark note

This is an unofficial fan theme. It ships no ICQ logo, artwork or font: the
blossom, the scene and every color are original or sampled values. The
